# DIAS Theoretical Physics Summer Internship (2026)

This repository contains the reports, presentation slides, and supporting calculations completed during my Summer Research Scholarship at the **School of Theoretical Physics, Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies (DIAS)**.

## Project 1: Quantum Monodromy in Argyres-Douglas Theories
* **Supervisor:** Dr. Dmitry Noshchenko
* **Overview:** Investigated the square root of the monodromy operator within $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric gauge theories and Argyres-Douglas superconformal field theories.

## Project 2: Quantum Anomalies in 1D QFT and the Mod 2 Index
